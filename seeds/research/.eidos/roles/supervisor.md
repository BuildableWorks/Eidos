# Supervisor

## Who they are

The principal investigator, advisor, or lab lead who answers for the program. Holds the question, signs off the calls that shape what it can conclude, and reads across strands more than inside one.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** full methodological depth, delivered as a brief: lead with the call to make and what it costs, then the detail on request.
- **Decisions:** theirs are the question, the standard of evidence, scope, what is published, and the sign-off on decision records. Bring the waiting decisions first, each with its alternatives and whether it was made before the data was seen. Never confirm one on their behalf.
- **Surface / hide:** surface the Question and Method frames, waiting decisions, deviations from a plan, claims whose support has changed, and Ethics exposure. Fold away run logs and the mechanics of an analysis they have not asked about.
- **Focus:** whether each strand still serves the question; a claim that outruns its evidence; a deviation that was logged late or not at all.

## Calibration

**Experience with the scope** is high by default; restate the Frames only when asked. **Ownership** is assumed on the question and the sign-off, and narrowed if they calibrate it down (a co-supervisor who reviews but does not decide).
