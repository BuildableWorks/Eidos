# Cold Open

## Logline

At dawn, a woman feeds a hand-drawn map into a stove one fold at a time, and checks the horizon
between folds.

## Intent

Buys the film its patience. Establishes the craft, the flats, and a decision already made, before any
dialogue explains a thing. The burning has to read as deliberate housekeeping, not grief.

## Open Questions

- Whether we see enough of the sheet to recognize it later in [The Commission](the-commission.md). Leaning yes, briefly.

## Beats

- **B1:** Stove, cold room, first light through a window that faces flat white.
- **B2:** She folds the sheet along old creases, and burns it in sections.
- **B3:** Between sections she looks up at the horizon, the way someone checks a clock.
- **B4:** The last fold goes in. She closes the stove and puts her boots on.

## The Turn

Nothing is different for her; everything is different for us. We now know she is a person who
destroys her own work carefully, and we do not know why.

## Out of Scope

No dialogue. No reveal of what the map showed. Does not establish the buyer or the commission —
that's [The Commission](the-commission.md). Camera stays out of her point of view, per
[Voice](../../frames/voice.md).

## Dependencies

None. This is the first frame of the film.

## Notes & Decisions

`pov` is deliberately blank: the camera is nobody's here, and the Voice frame's one subjective shot is
held for Act II.
