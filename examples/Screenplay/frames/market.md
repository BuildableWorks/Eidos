# Market

## Format & Length

Fifteen minutes. Short enough to be programmed, long enough for two takes of a crossing that has to
feel real.

## Where It Plays

Regional festivals first, then the two shorts programmes that take patient work. No platform strategy
beyond that; the film is a calling card for a feature on the same subject.

## Comparables

- *The Rider* — patience with labor. This is shorter and colder.
- *First Cow* — a transaction carrying the feeling. This has one transaction, not a friendship.
- *Leviathan* — texture over exposition. This keeps a plot; that one doesn't.

## What Success Looks Like

Selection at two festivals with a shorts jury, and one producer conversation about the feature. Not
views.
