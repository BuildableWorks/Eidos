# Audience

## Who Watches

Festival short-film audiences, and the crossover viewer who chose *The Rider* on a streaming service
and finished it. People who have watched a parent lose a skill.

## What They Bring

They expect a slow film to eventually state its subject. This one doesn't, and the Voice rules exist
to hold that line when a note asks for a scene of explanation.

## What Changes For Them

They spend fifteen minutes inside a competence failing, without being told that is what they are
watching, and recognize it afterward.

## Who It Is Not For

Anyone who needs the condition named. That viewer will be frustrated at minute nine, and losing them
is what lets the rest of it work.
