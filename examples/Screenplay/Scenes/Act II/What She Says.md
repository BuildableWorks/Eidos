# What She Says

## Logline

At the water's edge, Mira answers the buyer's question, or doesn't.

## Intent

The ending. It exists as a `beat` on purpose: the shape of the film is settled and this scene is not,
and writing a full definition for it now would fake a decision that hasn't been made. It stays here,
named and thin, rather than living in a notebook.

## Out of Scope

Whatever the ending turns out to be, it is not a scene where she explains her condition — that is out
of scope for the whole film, per [Premise](../../Frames/Premise.md).
