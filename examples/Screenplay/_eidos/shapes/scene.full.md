## Logline

_One sentence: what happens in this scene, from the outside. What a viewer would say it was about. This is the line the canvas card shows, so make it carry the scene alone._

## Intent

_Why this scene exists — the work it does that no other scene does. This is the stable part: if Intent changes substantially, you have a different scene, not an edit to this one._

## Open Questions

_What you don't yet know about this scene. When one is settled it graduates into a beat, a Decision, or the Turn._

## Beats

_What happens, in order, as observable events. Label each **B1:**, **B2:**, … Keep each short and checkable against a draft — dialogue and staging belong in the script, not here._

## The Turn

_What is different at the end of this scene that wasn't true at the start — for the character, or for what the audience knows. A scene without a turn is usually two scenes or none._

## Out of Scope

_What this scene deliberately does not do, and which scene does it instead. The hardest-working section: it is what keeps a film from explaining itself three times._

## Dependencies

_What a viewer must already have seen for this scene to land. The `depends_on` property at the top is this as links._

## Notes & Decisions

_**Notes**: production constraints, references, things to watch. **Decisions**: an append-only log, one line each, dated._
