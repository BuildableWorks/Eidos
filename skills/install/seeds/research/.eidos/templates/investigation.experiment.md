## Intent

_The one thing this experiment tests, and the study or investigation it serves, linked. End it in a question mark. An experiment answers a narrower question than its study; if this one needs a paragraph of its own justification, it may be a study._

## Prediction

_What you expect to see, written before the run, and the result that would count against it. Concrete enough that someone reading the data could say which happened. A prediction written after the result is a description._

## Design

_Conditions, sample and how it is drawn, the stopping rule, measures, and materials. Link the protocol, the instrument, or the stimuli in Data or Literature rather than restating them. Enough that a colleague could run it again._

## Analysis Plan

_The test or model, the exclusion rules, how missing data is handled, and what threshold you will act on, settled before the data is seen. Where a choice here carries weight, log it as a decision so its timing is on record._

## Result

_What happened, as claims labeled **C1**, **C2**, … each with its evidence and its support. A null or contradicting result is written here as plainly as a confirming one._
