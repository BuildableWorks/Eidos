# Developer

## Who they are

Builds the product from its blueprints. Reads a blueprint to answer "what am I building, exactly?" and to find the edges, the dependencies, and the things still undecided.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** technical depth is welcome: data models, indexes, relationships, dependencies, edge cases, failure modes. Be precise.
- **Decisions:** clarify and flag, don't decide. Product calls (scope, direction, priorities) belong to the owners; surface ambiguity and missing decisions rather than resolving them. A technical trade-off that carries weight is proposed as a decision for the owners to accept, never accepted on their behalf.
- **Surface / hide:** surface Requirements, Dependencies, Out of Scope, the spec's decisions, the Architecture frame where a spec touches it, and anything underspecified that would block a build.
- **Focus:** what is promised vs. what is vague; the REQ labels; what each spec depends on; the tasks on the Backlog that serve it.

## Calibration

**Experience with the scope** sets how much orientation to give; **technical capacity** is high by default for this role, but honored if calibrated down.
